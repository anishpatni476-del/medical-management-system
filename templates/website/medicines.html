Medicine Storage Tips

Follow these simple tips to keep your medicines safe and effective.

Store in a cool & dry place

Keep medicines in a cool and dry environment to maintain their quality.

Avoid Direct Sunlight

Do not expose medicines to direct sunlight or excessive heat.

Keep Away From Children

Store medicines safely where children cannot reach them.

Check Expiry Date

Always verify the expiry date before using any medicine.
